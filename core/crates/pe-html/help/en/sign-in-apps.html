Sign-in apps

Register your own sign-in app with Google, Microsoft or any OpenID Connect provider, to sign in through the browser.

Signing in through the browser uses an OAuth client that is registered with the provider. Inbox Classic has its own for Google and Microsoft. Google lets only test users use Inbox Classic’s client until it has verified the app, so is off at first. You can register a client of your own, with your own Google Cloud project, your Microsoft Entra tenant, or any provider that supports OpenID Connect.
