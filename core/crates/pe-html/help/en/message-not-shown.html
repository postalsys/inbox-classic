A message is not shown

What to do when a message is still downloading, cannot be downloaded, or cannot be shown.

“Downloading the message…”

A message is downloaded the first time you open it, so right after you add an account, most messages are not on your computer yet. The sender and subject show at once, and the message follows in a moment.

“The message could not be downloaded”

The pane says why, for example that the mail server could not be reached. The message is downloaded by itself once the account is back online. To try at once, click the bar over the messageclick .

“This message can not be shown”

Some messages are damaged or built in a way no mail program can read. Inbox Classic shows this note instead of the message. The rest of your mail is not affected. To see what the message holds, choose > .
