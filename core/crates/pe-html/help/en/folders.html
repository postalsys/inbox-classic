Work with folders

Make, rename and delete folders of your own on the mail server.

To make a folder:

  1. Select the folder to make it in. To make it at the top of an account, select the account.To make it at the top of an account, right-click the account’s name instead.
  2. Choose > > > , or right-click and choose .
  3. Type the name and click . The folder is made on the server.

To rename the selected folder, choose > > > . To delete it, choose > > > . Both are also on the folder’s right-click menu.

Deleting a folder deletes the messages and folders in it, on the server too. Inbox Classic asks first.

The Inbox and the special folders, such as , and , cannot be renamed or deleted.

To open a folder without the mouse, choose > () and type part of its name.

The folder list remembers which folders you opened and closed.
