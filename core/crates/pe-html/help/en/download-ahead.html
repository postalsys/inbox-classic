Messages downloaded ahead

Download message contents in advance so they can open quickly and be read offline.

Depending on your settings, Inbox Classic downloads message contents in the background while connected. Downloaded messages can open quickly, including offline. Opening a message can also download its date-neighbors in the same folder, subject to the size setting.

To choose what is downloaded ahead, choose :

  • : downloads eligible messages from the last 30 days, up to 1 MB each. includes older and larger messages, subject to the limit below. disables these advance downloads, including neighboring messages.
  • controls cached message contents per account. Older downloaded contents can be removed locally and fetched again when needed; messages stay on the server. Drafts and messages opened during this run are kept, so the limit can be exceeded. It does not limit the whole mail database.

Messages in and , and Gmail’s All Mail, are excluded from bulk advance downloads. Opening a message can still fetch its contents.

A message over 1 MB can show its text before the full download finishes, if its structure allows this. Its attachments follow, and the status bar says how far they are. They open, and the message can be saved or forwarded, once it is all here.
