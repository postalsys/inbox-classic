Messages downloaded ahead

New mail is downloaded as it arrives, so it opens at once.

Inbox Classic downloads new messages in the background, as they arrive. Opening one then shows it at once, also without a connection. When you open a message, the messages above and below it are downloaded too, for the next one you open.
