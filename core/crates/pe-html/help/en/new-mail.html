Get new mail

How Inbox Classic checks for mail, tells you about it, and keeps running when its window is closed.

Inbox Classic checks every account when it starts and every ten minutes after that. It also watches each Inbox, so new mail shows up as it arrives.

Notifications

While the window is not in front, a notification says who wrote and about what. Click it to open the message. To turn notifications off or on, choose > . The same switch is in > Inbox Classic > > .

A new message in a conversation you watch is always announced. One in a conversation you ignore never is.

When you close the window
