Get new mail

How Inbox Classic checks for mail, tells you about it, and keeps running when its window is closed.

Inbox Classic checks every account when it starts and every ten minutes after that. While connected, it also watches each Inbox for changes. How quickly new mail appears depends on the server and connection.

Notifications

While the window is not in front, a notification says who wrote and about what. Click it to open the message. To turn notifications off or on, choose > . The same switch is in .

Watched conversations can trigger notifications for new mail in the Inbox or ordinary user folders. Notification settings and foreground suppression still apply. Ignored conversations and special folders such as Sent Items, Drafts, Junk E-mail and Deleted Items do not trigger these notifications.
