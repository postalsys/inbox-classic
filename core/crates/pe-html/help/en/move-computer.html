Move to another computer

Take your accounts, settings, contacts, keys and rules to another computer in one file.

Export them

  1. Choose > .
  2. Choose what to take: , , , , , , and .
  3. To include saved passwords, sign-in credentials and private keys, turn on and type a passphrase of at least 8 characters twice.
  4. Click and choose where to save the file.

The file contains no messages: they download again from the server on the other computer. A passphrase encrypts the whole file. Without one, saved passwords, sign-in credentials and private keys are left out. You will need to sign in to your accounts again.

Keep the file and its passphrase apart, and delete the file once you have imported it. Anyone with both can read your mail.

Import them

  1. On the other computer, choose > . Before you add your first account, has a link for it too.
  2. Choose the file, and type its passphrase if it has one.
  3. The window shows what the file holds. Choose the parts and accounts to import, and click .

Accounts already configured on this computer stay unchanged. Contacts are added to your address book; settings and window preferences are replaced. Missing destination folders for rules are matched after the accounts sync. A new key for a contact replaces the existing one only when you select it in the contact’s properties.
