Move to another computer

Take your accounts, settings, contacts, keys and rules to another computer in one file.

Export them

  1. Choose > .
  2. Choose what to take: , , , , , , and .
  3. To include the selected accounts’ accessible sign-in credentials and your selected accessible private keys, turn on and type a passphrase of at least 8 characters twice.
  4. Click and choose where to save the file.

The file contains no messages: mail still on the server downloads again on the other computer. The local Outbox is not transferred. A passphrase encrypts the whole file. Private keys that cannot be unlocked are omitted. Without one, saved passwords, sign-in credentials and private keys are left out. You will need to sign in to your accounts again.

Keep the file and its passphrase apart, and delete the file once you have imported it. Anyone with both can read your mail.

Import them

  1. On the other computer, choose > . Before you add your first account, has a link for it too.
  2. Choose the file, and type its passphrase if it has one.
  3. The window shows what the file holds. Choose the parts and accounts to import, and click .

Accounts already configured on this computer stay unchanged. Contacts are merged into your address book, including groups; selected settings and window preferences replace the current ones. Inbox Classic tries to match missing rule destinations after the accounts sync; it does not create those folders. A new key for a contact replaces the existing one only when you select it in the contact’s properties.
