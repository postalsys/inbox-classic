Flag, archive and move messages

Flag messages, mark them as junk, archive them or move them to another folder, one or several at a time.

Select several messages

Use Ctrl+Click⌘+Click to add a message to the selection, or click the first and Shift+Click the last. > () selects every message of the list. The commands below then work on all of them.
