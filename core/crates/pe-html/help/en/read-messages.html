Read messages

Read a message in the reading pane or in a window of its own, and move through your mail with the keyboard.

Select a message in the list to see it in the reading pane, under its sender, date, recipients and subject. A message is marked read after it has been shown for a second. The list shows each message’s date, but never one later than when it arrived, so mail dated in the future does not stay at the top.

Dark mode

When your Mac is set to dark, messages are shown on a dark background too, and so is the text you write. Messages that bring colors of their own, such as newsletters and receipts, stay light, as they were designed. Printed messages are always light.
