Conversations

Messages that answer each other are grouped into one row, and you can watch or ignore a conversation.

A collapsed conversation has one row. It shows the subject of the conversation, the number of its messages in the current list and how many of them are new, as in (3, 1 new). The sender and first words are those of the message it opens: the oldest unread one, or the newest when all are read. It is bold while any of its messages is unread. Date and received-date sorts use its newest message; other sorts use its first message.

The whole conversation in the reading pane

Select a conversation’s row to read its messages together, starting at its oldest unread message, or its newest when all are read. Over them are the conversation’s subject, how many messages and unread messages it has, and who wrote in it. Each open message is in a frame of its own, with its sender, who it went to, when it was sent, and its own buttons. Each folded message is a row with its sender, its first words and its date; a paper clip means it has attachments. Initially, unread messages and the starting message are open, and other read messages are folded.

  • Click a folded row to open that message. Click an open message, anywhere in its frame, to select it; it stays open. The arrow at the start of a row or frame opens or folds that message.
  • Reply, Reply All, Forward, Print and the bars over the stack act on the selected message, the one with the colored edge. Each frame also has its own , and buttons, and with Print, Mark as Unread, Delete, Open and View Source for that message. A draft has to go on writing it.
  • Your own messages are named . Your replies in Sent Items, your drafts and messages you filed in other folders are shown too, each with the name of its folder. Messages in Junk E-mail and Deleted Items are left out.
  • In a long conversation, four or more read messages in a row between the first message and the last two read ones are folded into one row that says how many there are. Click it to list them all. The pane opens at the starting message.
  • > () and () open and select the next or previous message. opens every message, and folds every message but the selected one. With the stack in front, → and ← do the same.
  • The unread messages shown open are marked read after a moment.
  • If an open message has pictures from the internet that are not shown, the bar over the stack offers them. Pictures in encrypted messages load only when you ask for them in that message.
  • A signed or encrypted message is shown in the stack like any other, with its security labels.
  • > prints downloaded messages in the conversation, one after another. Signed or encrypted messages are left out; open and print those separately.
  • Double-click a row or a frame to open that message in a window of its own.

A single message, in no conversation, is shown in a frame of its own in the same way.

Watch a conversation

Select a message and choose > , or press W in the message list. New messages in that conversation can trigger notifications from the Inbox or ordinary user folders. Notifications must be enabled, and the main window must not be in front. Special folders such as Sent Items, Drafts, Junk E-mail and Deleted Items do not trigger watched-conversation notifications. The command is checked while you watch it.

Ignore a conversation

Choose > , or press K in the message list. The conversation turns gray and is marked read. New messages in it arrive read, without a notification. To leave ignored conversations out of the list, choose > .

To take a mark away, choose the same command again. Both commands are also on the right-click menu of a message.
