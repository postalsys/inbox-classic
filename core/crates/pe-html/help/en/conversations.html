Conversations

Messages that answer each other are grouped into one row, and you can watch or ignore a conversation.

A conversation is listed once, on its first message, with the number of messages it has. It is bold while one of its messages is unread, and it is placed by its newest message.

  • To see its other messages, click the +▸ in front of it, or press →. To hide them, click the -▾, or press ←.
  • > and do the same for the whole list.
  • Delete, move, archive, junk, mark read and flag on a collapsed conversation act on all of its messages.

To list every message on a row of its own, turn off in > Inbox Classic > > .

Watch a conversation

Select a message and choose > , or press W in the message list. A new message in the conversation is then always announced, even when it arrives in a folder other than the Inbox. The command is checked while you watch it.

Ignore a conversation

Choose > , or press K in the message list. The conversation turns gray and is marked read. New messages in it arrive read, without a notification. To leave ignored conversations out of the list, choose > .

To take a mark away, choose the same command again. Both commands are also on the right-click menu of a message.
