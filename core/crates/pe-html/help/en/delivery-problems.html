Messages that were not delivered

When Inbox Classic recognizes a delivery failure or delay notice, it explains the reported reason and what you can do.

A delivery notice has an undelivered mark in the message list. When you open it, a card above it says whether your message was not delivered or is only delayed.

The card lists each recipient with the reason in plain words and what you can do. shows the receiving server’s own words.

  • selects the original message if it can be found in the account.
  • For a delay, selects the original message if available. The notice reports that delivery is still being tried; wait for the outcome before sending again.

Some common reasons and advice for a final failure. If delivery is only delayed, wait for the outcome before sending again:

The card saysWhat to do
Check the spelling of the address, or ask the person for the one they use now.
Reach the person another way and ask them to make space before you try again.
Check the server’s explanation. If it rejected the content, revise the message before trying again.
Forward the notice to your mail provider. The sending server’s address was reported as blocked.
The receiving server asked for a later attempt. A delay notice does not guarantee when delivery will succeed.

To find every delivery notice, search for is:bounce. The notice is analyzed on your computer; its contents are not sent to a separate analysis service.
