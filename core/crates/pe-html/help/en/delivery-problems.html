Messages that were not delivered

When a message you sent bounces or is delayed, Inbox Classic explains why and what you can do.

A delivery notice has an undelivered mark in the message list. When you open it, a card above it says whether your message was not delivered or is only delayed.

The card lists each recipient with the reason in plain words and what you can do. shows the receiving server’s own words.

To find every delivery notice, search for is:bounce. The reason is worked out on your computer, and nothing is sent anywhere.
