Verify a contact’s key

Compare a key’s fingerprint with its owner to be sure the key is really theirs.

A key can come from anywhere: a message, a key server, a file. Anyone can make a key with your contact’s name and address. Verifying confirms that the key you have is the one your contact has.

To verify a key:

  1. Choose > , select the contact’s key and click . You can also click it in the contact’s properties in the Address Book.
  2. Ask your contact to read the fingerprint from their own app, in person or on a call. Also check that every listed address belongs to them.
  3. If every group of four matches and all the addresses belong to your contact, click . The key shows as verified.
  4. If any listed address does not belong to your contact, even if the fingerprints match, click instead.
Do not compare the fingerprint with one sent by email. Whoever could change the key in a message could change the fingerprint too.

If the fingerprints differ, click . The key you have is not your contact’s: remove it, and ask your contact to send their key again.

Valid signatures from the sender show in green if you have verified their key, or blue if you have not. An unverified key can still be used for encryption.
