The password is not accepted

What to do when the mail server refuses your password or sign-in.

A warning appears beside the account in the folder list, or beside its Inbox if you have only one account. Point at it for details. The bar above the message list explains what to do. The status bar and tray iconDock icon also show the problem.

Inbox Classic avoids repeating the same prompt. Automatic retries become less frequent to reduce rejected sign-in attempts. Click to try now.

An account that uses a password

  1. Click the warning icon or in the bar, then enter the password again. You can also change it in > , in the account’s properties.
  2. If your provider requires an app password, create one in its security settings and use that instead.
  3. For Gmail, an app password requires 2-Step Verification and may not be available for your account. Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 require browser sign-in in Inbox Classic. See .

An account that signs in through the browser

When Inbox Classic asks “”, choose and sign in again. If you choose , you can sign in later using the warning icon or in the bar. You can also use > or the account’s properties.

Your provider or organization may require a new sign-in, for example after a password change.

  • If administrator approval is required, choose and send the link to your administrator. Signing in again cannot replace that approval.
  • If IMAP is turned off, enable it in your mailbox’s settings or ask your administrator.
  • If you signed in with another address, choose and select the correct account on the provider’s page.
  • During a temporary provider outage, Inbox Classic waits and retries without asking you to sign in.
