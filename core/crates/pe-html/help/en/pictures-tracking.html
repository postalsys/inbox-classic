Pictures and tracking

Why pictures from the internet are blocked, and how to show them for a message or a sender you trust.

A message can show pictures the sender keeps on the internet. Downloading such a picture tells the sender that you opened the message, when, and from where. Inbox Classic blocks them by default.

Pictures sent inside the message are shown, as they tell the sender nothing.

Inbox Classic converts supported embedded pictures into static images, as it does for attachment previews. A picture it cannot draw (such as HEIC) shows as blank space. Animated pictures show their first frame. Fonts sent with a message are not used.

When a message has pictures from the internet, a bar across it says some pictures have been blocked. To see them for a message you trust:

  • Click on the bar.
  • Or choose > .

This permits pictures for the current message. Other messages follow your automatic-loading and trusted-sender settings.

Senders you trust

To get a sender’s pictures every time, click the arrow beside on the bar. Choose to always download pictures from that sender, or from everyone at their domain.

Their pictures then load by themselves, but only when your mail provider confirms the message really comes from that domain. A message without domain confirmation still asks, and the bar explains that it could not confirm the domain. Domain confirmation does not identify the individual sender. In and encrypted message parts, remote pictures still require an explicit request.

Pictures in every message

in allows automatic remote pictures except in and encrypted message parts. It is off at first, because senders can then tell when you read their messages.

A brand logo beside the header is separate from the message’s remote pictures. Your mail server approves it, and Inbox Classic caches it. A logo download or refresh still contacts its server.
