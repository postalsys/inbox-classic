Pictures and tracking

Why pictures from the internet are blocked, and how to show them for a message you trust.

A message can show pictures the sender keeps on the internet. Downloading such a picture tells the sender that you opened the message, when, and from where. So Inbox Classic does not download them.

Pictures sent inside the message are shown, as they tell the sender nothing.

When a message has pictures from the internet, a bar across it says some pictures have been blocked. To see them for a message you trust:

  • Click the bar.Click on the bar.
  • Or choose > .

This shows the pictures of that message only. The next message starts blocked again.

A brand logo beside the header is not a tracking picture. It is approved by your mail server, and it comes from the brand, not from the sender’s pictures.
