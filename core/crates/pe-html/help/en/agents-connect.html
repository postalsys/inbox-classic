Connect an AI agent

Let an AI agent, such as Claude, read and organize your mail through Inbox Classic.

If you use an AI agent, such as Claude, Cursor or another program that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), you can let it work with your mail. There is no AI in Inbox Classic, and nothing is sent to an AI service unless you let an agent in.

To connect an agent:

  1. Choose > and click . Inbox Classic shows the lines to add to your agent’s settings.
  2. Add them to the MCP servers of your agent. In Claude Desktop, that is Settings > Developer > Edit Config.
  3. Start the agent. The first time it connects, Inbox Classic asks you what it may do.

The agent starts Inbox Classic in the background if it is not running. Only programs running as you on this computer can reach it.

To turn all agents off at once, clear in > .
