Connect an AI agent

Let an AI agent, such as Claude, read and organize your mail through Inbox Classic.

You can let an AI agent work with your mail. It can be Claude, Cursor or another program that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP). There is no AI in Inbox Classic, and nothing is sent to an AI service unless you let an agent in.

To connect an agent:

  1. Choose > and click . Inbox Classic shows the lines to add to your agent’s settings.
  2. Add them to the MCP servers of your agent. In Claude Desktop, that is Settings > Developer > Edit Config.
  3. Start the agent. The first time it connects, Inbox Classic asks you what it may do.

The agent starts Inbox Classic in the background if it is not running. Only programs running as you on this computer can reach it.

Agents you start from a terminal, such as Claude Code, are one program to Inbox Classic. It asks once for programs started from Terminalprograms started from a terminal, and they share what you allow. To give an agent its own access, add --name and a name of your choice after --mcp in its settings, for example "args": ["--mcp", "--name", "Claude Code"]. Inbox Classic then asks about it by that name.

To turn all agents off at once, turn off in > .
