Search words

The words and signs the search box understands, to find exactly the messages you want.

The search box takes the same words as Gmail’s. Combine as many as you like: a message must match all of them. Point at the search box to see a short list.

TypeFinds
budgetMessages with a word that starts with budget, anywhere in them
"exact phrase"These words, in this order
-wordMessages without the word. A minus works before any search word, such as -is:unread
this OR thatMessages with either word. Write OR in capitals
from:annMail from Ann
to:ann, cc:, bcc:Mail to Ann, in any recipient line
subject:lunchThe word in the subject
filename:pdfThe word in the name of an attached file
list:news.example.comMail of a mailing list, by its List-Id
has:attachmentMessages with files
is:unread, is:readUnread or read messages
is:starred or is:flaggedFlagged messages
is:answeredMessages you replied to
is:bounceNotices of messages that were not delivered or are delayed
in:inbox, in:sent, in:drafts, in:trash, in:spam, in:archiveOnly that folder, in every account
in:projectsOnly the folder with that name
in:anywhereEvery folder
before:2025/01/31, after:2025/01/01Mail before or after a day. 2025-01-31 works too
older_than:2d, newer_than:1mMail older or newer than days (d), months (m) or years (y)
larger:5M, smaller:100kMail larger or smaller than a size, in bytes or with K, M or G

Accents and capital letters do not matter. A search word Inbox Classic does not know is searched for as a word.

The list shows no sizes. To see how big a message is, choose > .
