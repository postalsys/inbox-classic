Search words

The words and signs the search box understands, to find exactly the messages you want.

The search box supports the Gmail-style terms listed below. Combine as many as you like: a message must match all of them. Point at the search box to see a short list.

TypeFinds
budgetMessages with a word that starts with budget, anywhere in them
"exact phrase"These words, in this order
-wordMessages without the word. A minus also negates supported filters, such as -is:unread, but not date, age or size filters
this OR thatMessages with either word. Write OR in capitals
from:annMail from Ann
to:ann, cc:, bcc:Mail to Ann in any recipient line; these three operators search the same recipient index
subject:lunchThe word in the subject
filename:pdfThe word in the name of an attached file
list:news.example.comMail from a mailing list, identified by its List-Id
has:attachmentMessages with files
is:unread, is:readUnread or read messages
is:starred or is:flaggedFlagged messages
is:answeredMessages you replied to
is:bounceNotices of messages that were not delivered or are delayed
is:encrypted, is:signedEncrypted or signed messages
in:inbox, in:sent, in:drafts, in:trash, in:spam, in:archiveOnly that folder, in every account
in:projectsOnly the folder with that name
in:anywhereEvery folder
before:2025/01/31, after:2025/01/01Mail before the start of a day or on and after that start, in UTC. 2025-01-31 works too
older_than:2d, newer_than:1mMail older or newer than the given interval, measured in days (d), 30-day months (m) or 365-day years (y)
larger:5M, smaller:100kMail larger or smaller than a size, in bytes or with K, M or G

Accents and letter case do not matter. Unquoted terms of at least two characters match word beginnings; a single character matches a whole word. An unrecognized search operator is treated as ordinary text.

An encrypted message can be found by its outer sender, recipients and subject. Decrypted text and protected subjects become searchable after indexing with available keys, while is on under .

If the current layout does not show message sizes, choose > .
