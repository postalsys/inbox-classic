Emoji reactions

See who reacted to your messages with an emoji in Gmail or Outlook, without the reaction emails in your Inbox.

When someone reacts to a message with an emoji in Gmail or Outlook, their mail program sends the reaction as an email. Inbox Classic shows the reaction on the message it is for and keeps that email out of your lists.

  • The message's row shows the reactions after its subject: up to three emoji and, when there are more, how many.
  • In the reading pane, under the header shows who reacted with what.
  • A new reaction comes as a notification, as new mail does.

The reaction email stays on your mail server, marked as read, so other mail programs still show it. To see it, right-click a reaction and choose . If it was not a reaction you wanted folded away, choose to list it as an ordinary email again.

Only a reaction from someone in the conversation, whose mail provider vouches for it, is shown this way. Anything else stays an ordinary email.
