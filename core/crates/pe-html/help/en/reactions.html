Emoji reactions

See who reacted to your messages with an emoji in Gmail or Outlook, without the reaction emails in your Inbox.

When someone reacts to a message with an emoji in Gmail or Outlook, their mail program sends the reaction as an email. Inbox Classic shows the reaction on the message it is for and keeps that email out of your lists.

  • The message’s row shows up to three different emoji after its subject, plus the total number of reactions when there is more than one.
  • In the reading pane, under the header shows who reacted with what.
  • New reactions can trigger notifications, following your new-mail notification settings.

The reaction email stays on your mail server, marked as read. Other mail programs may show it as a reaction or an email. To open the latest email behind a person’s reactions, right-click their reaction and choose . Choose to list that person’s reaction emails for this message as ordinary mail again.

A reaction is shown this way only if the original message is stored here, the sender is one of its participants, and trusted mail-server checks confirm the sending domain. This does not verify the sender’s personal identity. Reactions that do not meet these checks remain ordinary email.
