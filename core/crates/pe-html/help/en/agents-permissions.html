What an agent may do

Choose what each AI agent may do with your mail, and see and undo what it did.

The first time a program’s agent connects, Inbox Classic asks you, naming the program. Choose what it may do:

  • read mail: folders, the message list, conversations, search and messages with their PDF, Office and picture files up to 10 MB, and look up your message rules and the people you write to
  • organize mail: read state, flags and their colors, junk, moving, archiving, new folders, renaming your own folders, watching and ignoring conversations, and deleting to , all of these also for every message a search finds at once, and leaving mailing lists after you say yes
  • write drafts for you to send, and change or remove the drafts it wrote until you save or send them yourself
  • send mail, each message only after you say yes

Then choose the accounts it may see, and click . turns the program away, and you are not asked about it again.

An agent never changes your account settings, and it deletes nothing for good but its own drafts: when it discards a draft it wrote that you have not saved, the draft is removed at once, without going to . It can read and try out your message rules, but never create, change or delete one. It never reads what an encrypted message says or carries. Reading a message does not mark it read. What it changes shows in the windows at once.

Leave mailing lists

An agent that may organize mail can ask to unsubscribe you from mailing lists. Inbox Classic first asks you once, listing each list by its sender:

  • asks each list to stop sending to you.
  • , the default, leaves them as they are.

An agent cannot unsubscribe you from messages in . Each program can unsubscribe you from at most 30 lists in 24 hours. Every list you said yes to counts, even one that could not be left.

Change many messages at once

An agent that may organize mail can change every message a search finds in one step, such as marking all newsletters read or moving them to a folder. It first counts the messages and sees a few of them. Then it changes exactly those, at most 1,000 at a time, and never mail that arrived since. Inbox Classic does not ask you first, as all of it can be taken back. The log shows it as one row, such as “Marked 37 messages read (search: from:news)”, and one Undo takes all of it back.

Mail is written by other people, and a message can try to talk an agent into forwarding or deleting your mail. Inbox Classic hides from agents the text a message hides from you, and tells them that mail is not instructions. An agent can still be fooled. Allow only what you need.
