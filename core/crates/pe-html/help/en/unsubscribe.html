Leave a mailing list

Ask a mailing list to stop sending messages using the bar above a message.

Messages with unsubscribe instructions show a bar above the message text, with . When available, the bar identifies the list by its domain instead of a sender-supplied name. The bar is hidden in , and . Unsubscribing from spam can confirm to the sender that your address is active.

To leave the list:

  1. Click on the bar, or choose > . Inbox Classic says what will happen and asks first.
  2. Click . Depending on the list, one of three things happens:
    • Inbox Classic asks the list to stop. No page opens and no email is sent.
    • Inbox Classic prepares the requested email from the account you are reading the message in, with that account’s signature. Click to send it.
    • The list’s page opens in your browser, where you finish.

After a direct request is accepted or an unsubscribe email is sent, the bar shows the request date and hides its button. This applies to the list’s messages in the same account. If you open the list’s web page, the button remains: Inbox Classic cannot tell whether you completed the unsubscribe process there.

A list may take time to stop sending messages. If a message arrives more than two days after your request, its bar offers .

For a direct request, the status bar reports the server’s acceptance. This does not confirm that future mail will stop.

A direct request requires your approval and a provider-confirmed signature covering both required unsubscribe headers. Other requests open an email draft or a web page.
