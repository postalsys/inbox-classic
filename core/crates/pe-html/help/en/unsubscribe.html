Leave a mailing list

Ask a mailing list to stop sending messages, from the bar over its messages.

A message of a mailing list that says how to leave it has a bar over its text, with the list’s domain and . The bar names the list only by its domain, never by a name the sender chose. Junk E-mail, Sent Items and Drafts show no bar: leaving a list from spam tells the sender that your address works.

To leave the list:

  1. Click on the bar, or choose > . Inbox Classic says what will happen and asks first.
  2. Click . Depending on the list, one of three things happens:
    • Inbox Classic asks the list to stop. No page opens and no email is sent.
    • Inbox Classic prepares the requested email from the account you are reading the message in, with that account’s signature. Click to send it.
    • The list’s page opens in your browser, where you finish.

Afterwards the bar says when you asked, on every message of that list, and offers nothing more. A request by email counts once it has been sent. For a list whose page you opened, the bar keeps its button, as Inbox Classic cannot see whether you finished there.

A list may take up to two days to stop. If one of its messages arrives later than that, the bar offers .

For a direct request, the status bar reports the server’s acceptance. This does not confirm that future mail will stop.

A direct request requires your approval and a provider-confirmed signature covering both required unsubscribe headers. Other requests open an email draft or a web page.
