Leave a mailing list

Ask a mailing list to stop sending messages with the Unsubscribe button.

A message of a mailing list that says how to leave it has at the right of its header.

To leave the list:

  1. Click . Inbox Classic says what will happen and asks first.
  2. Confirm. Depending on the list, one of three things happens:
    • Inbox Classic asks the list to stop. No page opens and no email is sent.
    • Inbox Classic prepares the requested email from the account you are reading the message in, with that account’s signature. Click to send it.
    • The list’s page opens in your browser, where you finish.

For a direct request, the status bar reports the server’s acceptance. This does not confirm that future mail will stop.

A direct request requires your approval and a provider-confirmed signature covering both required unsubscribe headers. Other requests open an email draft or a web page.
