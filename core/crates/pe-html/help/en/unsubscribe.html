Leave a mailing list

Stop the messages of a mailing list with the Unsubscribe button.

A message of a mailing list that says how to leave it has at the right of its header.

To leave the list:

  1. Click . Inbox Classic says what will happen and asks first.
  2. Confirm. Depending on the list, one of three things happens:
    • Inbox Classic asks the list to stop. No page opens and no message is sent.
    • Inbox Classic writes the message the list wants. Click to send it.
    • The list’s page opens in your browser, where you finish.

The status bar says when the list accepted the request.

Inbox Classic asks the list to stop on its own only when the list’s server signed the message, so a made-up message cannot use it to reach someone else.
