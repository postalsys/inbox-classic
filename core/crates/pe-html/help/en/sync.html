Sync your computers

Keep your accounts, keys, contacts, rules and settings the same on every computer, through a folder or storage you own.

Sync stores your setup in a location you choose: a folder that Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Nextcloud or Syncthing copies between your computers, or S3-compatible storage. The synced data is encrypted with your sync passphrase before it leaves the computer. Sync does not transfer messages, including drafts saved only on this computer.

Set up sync on your first computer

Keep the sync passphrase somewhere safe. Without it, no other computer can join.

  2. Choose and click , or choose and fill in its details. Click .
  3. Type a sync passphrase twice and a name for this computer. Choose what to sync, then click .

Add another computer

  1. On the other computer, open , click and choose the same folder or storage. Click .
  2. Enter the sync passphrase and a name for this computer. Choose what to sync, then click .

Existing data is merged with the synced data. Matching accounts and contacts become one. Added accounts download the messages available on their mail servers.

While it syncs

Each computer syncs every few minutes and when you click . When changes conflict, sync keeps one version and saves replaced values in for you to restore. Passwords, sign-in credentials and private keys are excluded from this history. When another computer removes an account, the main window says so.

To stop syncing a computer you no longer use, click and remove it. To stop syncing this computer, click : everything stays as it is.
