Sync your computers

Keep your accounts, keys, contacts, rules and settings the same on every computer, through a folder or storage you own.

Sync keeps a vault of your setup in a place you choose: a folder that Dropbox, OneDrive, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Nextcloud or Syncthing copies between your computers, or S3-compatible storage. Everything in it is encrypted with your sync passphrase before it leaves the computer. Your messages are not in it; they stay on your mail servers.

Set up sync on your first computer

  1. Open and click .
  2. Choose and click , or choose and fill in its details. Click .
  3. Type a sync passphrase twice and a name for this computer. Choose what to sync, then click .
Keep the sync passphrase somewhere safe. Without it, no other computer can join.

Add another computer

  1. On the other computer, open , click and choose the same folder or storage.
  2. Type the sync passphrase you chose on the first computer, and click .

What the computer has already and what the sync holds come together: an account or contact on both becomes one. Your messages download again from the servers.

While it syncs

Each computer syncs every few minutes and when you click . When the same thing was changed on two computers at the same time, the change made last is kept and the other one goes to , where you can restore it. When another computer removes an account, the main window says so.

To stop syncing a computer you no longer use, click and remove it. To stop syncing this computer, click : everything stays as it is.
