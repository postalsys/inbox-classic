Find messages

Search every folder of every account at once, as you type.

To find a message:

  1. Click the search box at the right of the toolbar, or press .
  2. Type a word from the message. The list shows the messages that match, from every folder, with a column that says where each one is.
  3. Select a result to read it. Reply, delete, move and flag work on it in its own folder.

A bar over the list says how many messages were found for which words.

The search looks in the subject, the people a message is from and to, the names of its files and its text. Words of at least two characters normally match the start of a word, so budg finds budget. A single character normally matches a whole word. Chinese, Japanese and Korean search terms can also match within longer words. Accents and capital letters do not matter.

To end the search and see the folder again, press Esc, click , empty the search box or choose a folder.

To search more precisely, such as only unread messages or only mail from one person, use search words like from: and is:unread. Point at the search box to see a list of them.

Search looks at the mail on this computer, so it works offline. While connected, Inbox Classic indexes message text in the background, newest first, without marking messages read. Subjects, senders, recipients and available file names become searchable after synchronization. Message text becomes searchable after indexing. Encrypted text also requires the encrypted-search setting and usable decryption keys.
