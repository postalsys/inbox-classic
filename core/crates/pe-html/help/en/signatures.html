Signatures

Give each account a signature that new messages, replies and forwards start with.

To add a signature:

  1. Choose > .
  2. Select the account and click .
  3. Write the signature. You can format it as you format a message, with bold, colors, links and a picture.
  4. Click .

New messages, replies and forwards from the account then start with the signature. It comes under an empty line to write on and the usual -- line, and above the quoted message.

When you choose another account in , that account’s signature takes the place of the first.

To remove a signature, open it again, delete its text and click .
