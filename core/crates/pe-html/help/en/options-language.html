Language and settings

Choose the language of Inbox Classic and how it shows and announces your mail.

Choose > Inbox Classic > > .

  • chooses the language of the windows and of this help. Choose the language of your system, or English, Deutsch, Eesti, Español or Français. When Inbox Classic does not have your system’s language, it uses English.
  • turns the notifications of new mail on or off.
  • groups messages that answer each other on one row.
  • shows the code of a sign-in message in its notification.

To change the language, Inbox Classic starts again. Click , or to change it the next time it starts.

The installer asks for the language on a first install, and keeps it as the language chosen here.

You can also choose the language for Inbox Classic alone in System Settings > General > Language & Region > Applications.
