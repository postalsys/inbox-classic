Settings

Choose the language of Inbox Classic, how it shows and announces your mail, and what it downloads in advance.

Choose . The settings are on three pages: , and . Click to keep your changes, or to keep them and leave the window open. undoes every change since then, including trusted senders you removed. An app you chose for .eml files in Windows settings stays chosen. Each change takes effect immediately, apart from the language, which changes when Inbox Classic starts again.

  • chooses the language of the windows and of this help: your system’s language, or one of the languages in the list. When Inbox Classic does not have your system’s language, it uses English.
  • shows a notification when new mail arrives. It is on at first.
  • shows the code of a sign-in message in its notification. It is off at first, as a notification can be read on a locked screen, and it is unavailable while notifications are off.
  • keeps a message you send in the Outbox for a few seconds, so you can still cancel it with Undo. It is 10 seconds at first.
  • opens e-mail links in Inbox Classic, and opens message files. See .
  • looks for a new version of Inbox Classic from time to time. See .

To change the language, Inbox Classic starts again. Click , or to change it the next time it starts.

  • chooses the layout: automatic by the window’s width, classic, or columns. See .
  • groups messages that answer each other on one row. It is on at first.
  • shows brand logos, contacts’ pictures and initials in the column layout. It is on at first.
  • shows the messages you read and write on a dark background while the system is in dark mode. It is on at first.
  • shows pictures and the first pages of PDF files after the text of a message. It is on at first. Turn off to show pictures but not PDF files. See .
  • downloads the pictures of every message. It is off at first, because senders can then tell when you read their messages. See .
  • lists the senders whose pictures are always downloaded, where you can remove them.
  • decrypts encrypted messages on this computer once they are downloaded, so search finds their words and the list shows their own subjects. It is on at first. Their text is kept unencrypted in the search index on this computer; turning this off removes it. AI agents never search it.
  • lets Inbox Classic ask keys.openpgp.org for the key of an address you encrypt to, after the address’s own domain. Only that address is sent, and only when you encrypt or look for a key. It is on at first.

  • chooses the messages downloaded before you open them: recent messages up to 1 MB each at first. See .
  • limits the space each account’s downloaded messages take. It is 1 GB at first.

The installer asks for the language on a first install, and keeps it as the language chosen here.

You can also choose the language for Inbox Classic alone in System Settings > General > Language & Region > Applications.
