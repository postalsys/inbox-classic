Settings

Choose the language of Inbox Classic, how it shows and announces your mail, and what it downloads in advance.

Choose . The settings are on three pages: , and . Click to keep your changes, or to keep them and leave the window open. discards changes made since opening the dialog or last applying them, including unapplied trusted-sender removals. An app you chose for .eml files in Windows settings stays chosen. Each change takes effect immediately, apart from the language, which changes when Inbox Classic starts again.

  • chooses the language of the windows and of this help: your system’s language, or one of the languages in the list. Inbox Classic tries the system’s preferred languages and falls back to English when none is supported.
  • allows new-mail notifications when the main window is not in front and the system permits them. It is on at first.
  • shows the code of a sign-in message in its notification. It is off at first, as a notification can be read on a locked screen, and it is unavailable while notifications are off.
  • delays the start of sending, so you can cancel with Undo before the countdown ends. It is 10 seconds at first.
  • lets you choose Inbox Classic for e-mail links, and for message files. See .
  • looks for a new version of Inbox Classic from time to time. See .

To change the language, Inbox Classic starts again. Click , or to change it the next time it starts.

  • chooses the layout: automatic by the window’s width, classic, or columns. See .
  • groups messages that answer each other on one row. It is on at first.
  • shows brand logos, contacts’ pictures and initials in the column layout. It is on at first.
  • shows the messages you read and write on a dark background while the system is in dark mode. It is on at first.
  • shows pictures and the first pages of PDF files after the text of a message. It is on at first. Turn off to show pictures but not PDF files. See .
  • allows automatic remote pictures except in Junk E-mail and encrypted message parts. It is off at first, because senders can then tell when you read their messages. See .
  • lists trusted sender addresses and domains, where you can remove them. Automatic loading requires domain confirmation and still excludes Junk E-mail and encrypted parts.
  • indexes downloaded encrypted messages when usable decryption keys are available, so search can find their text and protected subjects. It is on at first. Their text and protected subjects are kept unencrypted in the local index. Turning this off removes the decrypted index data and restores the outer subjects. Agent text searches exclude this decrypted index.
  • lets Inbox Classic ask keys.openpgp.org for the key of an address you encrypt to, after the address’s own domain. A lookup can send an address, fingerprint or key ID, depending on the request. It happens when you encrypt or look for a key. It is on at first.

  • chooses the messages downloaded before you open them: recent messages up to 1 MB each at first. See .
  • controls cached message contents per account, excluding drafts and messages opened during this run. It is not a hard limit on the database size. It is 1 GB at first.

The installer asks for the language on a first install, and keeps it as the language chosen here.

You can also choose the language for Inbox Classic alone in System Settings > General > Language & Region > Applications.
