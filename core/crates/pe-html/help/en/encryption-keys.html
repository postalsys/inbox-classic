Encryption keys

Make a key to sign your messages and receive encrypted mail, and keep your contacts’ keys.

Inbox Classic signs and encrypts mail with OpenPGP keys. Your own key has two halves: a public key that you give to others, and a private key that stays on your computer. Your contacts’ keys let you check their signatures and encrypt mail to them.

To make your key:

  1. Choose > , then click .
  2. Choose the account and click . The key is used for the account’s address and its other addresses.
  3. Click and choose a passphrase. Keep the file and the passphrase in different safe places.
Without a backup, your key cannot be recovered if this computer is lost or reset, and mail encrypted to it can no longer be read.

Keys stay protected without passphrases

Your private keys are protected by a key kept in the Credential Managerthe keychainthe system keyring, so you never type a passphrase to use them. You choose a passphrase only for a backup. If your key shows as locked, that protection was reset: click and choose its backup.

Adding keys

Click to add a key from a file, such as one a contact sent you or a backup of your own. A private key protected by a passphrase asks for it once. Inbox Classic then asks whether to use an imported key of yours for your addresses.

A contact’s key is used for their address from then on. If a different key for the same address turns up later, the current one stays in use until you choose the new one in the contact’s properties.

To share your public key, select it and click . You can also choose your account’s key in its properties, on the tab.

When you encrypt to someone whose key you do not have, Inbox Classic asks their address’s own domain for it, then keys.openpgp.org. Only the address is sent. To never ask keys.openpgp.org, turn off under .

Keys also travel with mail. Messages from an address with a key carry its public key, so other apps can encrypt to you, and Inbox Classic learns a contact’s key from their messages the same way. To stop sending yours, turn off on the account’s tab.
