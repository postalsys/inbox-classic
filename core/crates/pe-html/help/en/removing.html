Remove Inbox Classic

Remove Inbox Classic from your computer, with or without the mail it downloaded.

Uninstall Inbox Classic from Settings > Apps > Installed apps. It asks whether to remove the mail it downloaded too.

Quit Inbox Classic and move it from Applications to the Trash. The mail it downloaded stays in ~/Library/Application Support/Inbox Classic until you delete that folder too.

Remove the package with sudo apt remove inbox-classic, or with sudo pacman -R inbox-classic on Arch Linux and Omarchy. The mail it downloaded stays in ~/.local/share/inbox-classic until you delete that folder too.

The mail on your servers is never removed.
