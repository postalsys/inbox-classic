Remove Inbox Classic

Remove Inbox Classic from your computer, and choose whether to keep its local data.

Uninstall Inbox Classic from Settings > Apps > Installed apps. It asks whether to remove all local app data too, including accounts, mail, contacts, keys and settings.

Quit Inbox Classic and move it from Applications to the Trash. The mail it downloaded stays in ~/Library/Application Support/Inbox Classic until you delete that folder too.

Remove the package with sudo apt remove inbox-classic, with sudo dnf remove inbox-classic on Fedora, or with sudo pacman -R inbox-classic on Arch Linux and Omarchy. The mail it downloaded stays in ~/.local/share/inbox-classic until you delete that folder too.

These default data folders also contain contacts, keys and settings; deleting them removes all that local data. The location may differ if you changed it. Uninstalling does not remove mail from servers or necessarily remove credentials from the system’s password store.
