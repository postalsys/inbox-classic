Layouts

Choose the classic layout, with the message list above the message you read, or the column layout for a wide screen.

Inbox Classic has two layouts:

  • : the folder list on the left, the message list at the top right and the reading pane below it.
  • : the folder list, the message list and the reading pane side by side. Each message has three lines: who sent it and when, the subject, and the first words of the message, without the earlier messages it quotes.

With , the layout follows the width of the window: a wide window shows the column layout, a narrower one the classic layout. Out of the box, the layout is automatic.

To choose a layout:

  1. Choose > .
  2. Choose , or .

The same choice is in > Inbox Classic > > .

Sort the messages

Choose > and what to sort by, then whether to sort ascending or descending. In the column layout, the button above the messages says what they are sorted by; click it for the same choices. In the classic layout, you can also click a column heading.

The first words of a message appear once Inbox Classic has its text. For mail that was here before, it downloads the text in the background.
