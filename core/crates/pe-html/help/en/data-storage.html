Where your mail and passwords are kept

What Inbox Classic keeps on your computer, and where.

  • Downloaded mail and account settings are, by default, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\InboxClassic\store.db~/Library/Application Support/Inbox Classic/store.db~/.local/share/inbox-classic/store.db. Startup options or your environment can change this location. Synced mail stays on the server; the Outbox is local. Cached copies allow offline reading while they remain stored. The database and search index are not encrypted as a whole.
  • Passwords, browser sign-in tokens and sign-in app secrets normally use the Windows Credential Manager, under InboxClassic, encrypted for your Windows account.your login keychain, under InboxClassic, encrypted for your Mac account. Keychain Access shows them.your keyring (GNOME Keyring), under InboxClassic, encrypted for your login. Without a keyring, they are kept only until Inbox Classic quits. These credentials are not stored in the mail database. In memory-only mode, they last only until the app quits.
  • Encryption keys are kept with your downloaded mail. Your private keys are encrypted there with a key kept beside your passwords, so the mail file alone cannot open them.

The search, the explanation of bounced messages, the grouping into conversations and the finding of sign-in codes all run on your computer. These features do not send your mail content to an external service.
