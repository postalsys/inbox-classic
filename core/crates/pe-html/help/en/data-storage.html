Where your mail and passwords are kept

What Inbox Classic keeps on your computer, and where.

  • Downloaded mail and account settings are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\InboxClassic\store.db~/Library/Application Support/Inbox Classic/store.db~/.local/share/inbox-classic/store.db. Your mail stays on the server too. The copy here is for reading quickly and offline, and for search.
  • Passwords, browser sign-ins and the secrets of sign-in apps are in the Windows Credential Manager, under Inbox Classic, encrypted for your Windows account.your login keychain, under InboxClassic, encrypted for your Mac account. Keychain Access shows them.your keyring (GNOME Keyring), under InboxClassic, encrypted for your login. Without a keyring, they are kept only until Inbox Classic quits. They are never in the mail store.

The search, the explanation of bounced messages, the grouping into conversations and the finding of sign-in codes all run on your computer. Nothing of your mail is sent anywhere for them.
