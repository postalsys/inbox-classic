Certificate errors

What to do when Inbox Classic cannot confirm that it is talking to the real mail server.

You may see one of these:

  • In the status bar: “TLS error: …”
  • In the reading pane: “The connection to the mail server could not be made secure (…).”

Inbox Classic could not confirm that the server is the one it claims to be, so it did not send your password.

To fix it:

  1. Open the account’s properties in > .
  2. Check the server names. They must be the names the server’s certificate is made for. That is often your provider’s name, such as imap.provider.com, not your own domain.
  3. Check that your computer’s date and time are right.

On a network that inspects encrypted traffic, such as some office or school networks, ask its administrator.

There is no switch to accept any certificate. Accepting a false one would let someone on the network read your password and mail.
