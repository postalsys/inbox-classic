Gmail and Outlook.com

Use browser sign-in for Gmail, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365; eligible Google accounts can also use an app password.

Google and Microsoft do not let mail programs use your usual password. In Inbox Classic, use browser sign-in for Outlook.com and Microsoft 365. Google app passwords are an option only if your account allows them.

Sign in through your browser

Click or in . You type your password on Google’s or Microsoft’s own page, so Inbox Classic never sees it. Such an account always uses the provider’s own mail servers.

When the sign-in expires

If Google or Microsoft ends the sign-in, Inbox Classic says “Sign-in to … was rejected or the session has expired.” It asks whether to sign in again in the browser. Choose and sign in once more.
