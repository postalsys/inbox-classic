Gmail and Outlook.com

Gmail takes a browser sign-in or an app password; Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 take only a browser sign-in.

Google and Microsoft do not let mail programs use your usual password. Gmail has two ways in; Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 take only the browser sign-in.

When the sign-in expires

If Google or Microsoft ends the sign-in, Inbox Classic says “Sign-in to … was rejected or the session has expired.” It asks whether to sign in again in the browser. Choose and sign in once more.
