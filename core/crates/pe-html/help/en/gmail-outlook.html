Gmail and Outlook.com

Gmail, Outlook.com and their business versions take a browser sign-in or an app password, never your usual password.

Google and Microsoft do not let mail programs use your usual password. You have two ways in.

Sign in through your browser

Click or in . You type your password on Google’s or Microsoft’s own page, so Inbox Classic never sees it. Such an account always uses the provider’s own mail servers.

When the sign-in expires

If Google or Microsoft ends the sign-in, Inbox Classic says “Sign-in to … was rejected or the session has expired.” and asks whether to sign in again in the browser. Choose and sign in once more.
