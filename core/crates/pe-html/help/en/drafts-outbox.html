Drafts and the Outbox

Save a message to finish later, and see what waits to be sent.

Save a draft

Click , or press Ctrl+S⌘S. The message is kept in the account’s folder, on the server. Saving again replaces the earlier copy. The status bar of the message says when it was saved.

Inbox Classic also saves the message by itself a few seconds after you stop typing, and every half a minute while you go on. If Inbox Classic or your computer stops unexpectedly, the messages you were writing open again the next time it starts.

Closing a message with changes asks whether to save them:

  • saves it in .
  • throws the changes away. A copy that was only saved by itself goes from too.
  • goes back to the message.

To go on writing, double-click the draft in . It opens with its recipients, text and files. Once it is sent, the draft is removed.

A message you write is a window of its own, so you can read mail while it stays open. Closing Inbox Classic first closes the messages you are writing, asking about those with changes.

The Outbox

puts the message in the Outbox, and it goes after a few seconds, while you can still take it back (see below). The status bar of the main window says when it is sent.

When a message cannot go, for example without a connection, it stays in the Outbox. It goes with the next check for mail, so nothing you wrote is lost.

To see what waits, choose > . It lists each message with why it did not go. tries again at once, and you can delete a message there too.

Take back a message you sent

After you click , the message waits a few seconds before it goes, and the status bar counts down. To take it back, click beside the countdown, or choose > . The message opens again as it was, to change or to throw away.

To choose how long a message waits, or to send it at once, choose > Inbox Classic > > and set . If you quit Inbox Classic while a message waits, it goes right away.
