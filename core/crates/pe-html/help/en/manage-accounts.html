Remove an account

Removing an account deletes the mail downloaded for it and its saved sign-in credentials from this computer. It also deletes unsent messages in that account’s local Outbox, including messages with no server copy. The mail on the server stays.

To remove an account:

  1. Choose > .
  2. Select the account and click , or, on Windows, press Delete.
  3. Confirm. The account is removed from the folder list.
