Reply and forward

Answer the sender or everyone, or send a message on to someone else.

  • () prepares a reply to the Reply-To address, if present, or to the sender.
  • () prepares a reply to the sender or Reply-To address and the To and Cc recipients, excluding your own addresses.
  • () prepares a forward. You choose the recipients and send it.

A reply or forward goes from the account the original message is in, and from the address it was sent to when that is one of the account’s other addresses.

The original message

In a reply, the original message is folded away behind a ••• button under your text, so you see only what you write. It is included in the reply even while folded, unless you edit or remove it. To shorten it, click the button, or select all with Ctrl+A⌘A: the original then becomes part of your text.

In a forward, the original is in the text from the start, since it is what you send.

A reply follows the original’s format. A reply to a plain text message is plain text, with the original quoted with >, unless you format something.
