Sort new mail with rules

Have new mail moved, marked read, flagged or archived as it arrives, by what it matches.

A rule is a search, in the words of the search box, and what to do with the new mail of your Inbox that matches it. For example, from:news@example.com can move the newsletter to a folder of its own and mark it read.

To make a rule:

  1. Choose > , then click .
  2. Type a name, and in what the mail has to match, such as from:, subject: or list: words. The line under it says how many messages in the Inbox match now.
  3. Choose what to do: move it to a folder, mark it read, flag it, archive it, move it to Junk E-mail, delete it, or not notify you of it.
  4. Click .

You can also start a rule from what you see: > starts one for the sender of the message selected, or its mailing list, and on the bar over search results starts one with the words you searched for.

How rules work

Rules sort the new mail of your Inboxes as it arrives, while Inbox Classic runs, also in the notification area. They do not touch the mail already there, unless you check . Rules run in their order: each rule that matches a message does its part, and one that moves the message away is the last for it. Use the check box in the list to turn a rule off without deleting it.

Rules run on this computer. To sort mail while it is off, use the filters of your mail provider.
