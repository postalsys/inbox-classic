Calendar invitations

A message with a calendar invitation shows the event above its text, ready to open in your calendar.

A card shows the first recognized event in a message, with these details when present:

  • what it is: an invitation, an updated invitation, a cancellation or a response
  • when it is: local time for a known time zone, the time as written otherwise, or dates for an all-day event
  • where it is, who organizes it, and how many are invited
  • how it repeats, if it does

To accept or decline, click . The event opens in your calendar app, where you answer it.

Inbox Classic reports if opening fails. Check that a calendar app can open invitation files; opening can also fail when one is installed.

An AI agent you let send mail can also answer an invitation for you, but you must approve each reply. It goes to the invitation’s organizer, who may differ from the email sender. The local answer changes after the mail server accepts it, without confirmation from the organizer’s calendar.
